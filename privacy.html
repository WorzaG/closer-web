Closer

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Privacy First: Closer is a strictly 1-to-1 tactile micro-connection app for couples and close partners. We do NOT sell your data, we do NOT display ads, and we do NOT track your background location history.

1 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Closer ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to minimizing the data we collect to strictly what is needed to deliver real-time tactile signals between you and your paired partner.

2 Data We Collect & How We Use It

Closer is built on the principle of data minimization (compliant with Apple App Store Guidelines and GDPR/KVKK):

  • Display Name / Nickname: An arbitrary name or nickname chosen by you (e.g. "Can") used solely to identify you to your paired partner. No email or password is required to create a profile.
  • Location Data (Foreground Only): When granted, we access your device location only while the application is active in the foreground to calculate the relative physical distance between you and your paired partner (e.g. "12.4 km apart"). We do not record, log, track, or store historical GPS tracks or coordinates on our servers. Distance is computed transiently and in-memory.
  • Push Notification Token: If you enable push notifications, a unique Expo Push Token is saved to send you instant micro-signals (such as "❤️ Gizem misses you!" or "🫂 Gizem is sending a hug!") when the app is minimized.
  • Device & Presence Status: Ephemeral battery level and connection status (e.g. "Home", "Work", "On the go") shared exclusively in real-time with your paired partner so you feel connected.
  • Micro-Signals: Transient signal records (signal type, timestamp, hug duration in seconds) sent between you and your partner to calculate shared touch stats.

3 1-to-1 Architecture & No Third-Party Sharing

Closer does not have a public feed, search directory, or social network. Your pairing code is private and pairs you with exactly one person. We do not sell, rent, license, or disclose your personal information to third-party data brokers, marketers, or analytics trackers.

4 Data Storage & Security

All network communications between your mobile device and our backend servers utilize encrypted transport security (HTTPS/TLS and secure WebSockets). Real-time interactions are ephemeral and kept strictly within your private couple room.

5 Permanent Account & Data Deletion

Under Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) and global privacy laws, you have the full right to delete all your data at any time:

  • Inside Closer, open Settings and tap "Hesabımı Kalıcı Olarak Sil" (Permanently Delete My Account).
  • This action immediately dissolves your partner link, erases your user profile, revokes your pair code, and purges all associated micro-signal logs permanently from our database.

6 Children's Privacy

Closer is intended for general audiences and couples aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

7 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8 Contact Us

If you have any questions, inquiries, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at:

Email: support@closerapp.com

2 Toplanan Veriler ve Kullanım Amaçları

Closer, KVKK, GDPR ve Apple App Store veri minimizasyonu ilkelerine tam uyumlu olarak tasarlanmıştır:

  • Rumuz / Kullanıcı Adı: Partnerinizin sizi ekranda tanıması için belirlediğiniz serbest isim (örn. "Can"). Profil açmak için e-posta veya şifre zorunlu değildir.
  • Konum Bilgisi (Sadece Uygulama Açıkken): İzin verdiğiniz takdirde, konum bilgisine yalnızca uygulama açıkken (ön planda) partnerinizle aranızdaki fiziksel mesafeyi hesaplamak ("12.4 km uzakta") amacıyla başvurulur. Sunucularımızda geçmiş konum izi veya rota kayıtları tutulmaz. Mesafe anlık ve geçici olarak hesaplanır.
  • Anlık Bildirim İzni ve Token: Bildirim izni verdiğinizde, uygulama kapalıyken partnerinizden gelen mikro-sinyalleri (örn. "❤️ Gizem seni özledi!", "🫂 Gizem sana sarılıyor!") kilit ekranınıza ulaştırabilmek için benzersiz bir bildirim belirteci (Expo Push Token) kullanılır.
  • Cihaz ve Durum Bilgisi: Anlık batarya seviyesi ve durumunuz ("Evde", "İşte", "Yolda"), sadece eşleştiğiniz partnerinizin nabız küresinde gerçek zamanlı görüntülenmek üzere paylaşılır.
  • Mikro-Sinyal Kayıtları: Partnerinizle karşılıklı dokunuş istatistiklerinizi ("En Uzun Sarılma", "Son Dokunuş") görüntüleyebilmeniz için sinyal tipi ve süresi geçici olarak eşleştirilir.
